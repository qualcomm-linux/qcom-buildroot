The Firmware Replacement

PBL and XBL stay proprietary — they still load and authenticate the next stage. Everything after them is replaced with open firmware: TF-A, OP-TEE, KVM, and U-Boot / UEFI.

PAS / PIL — authenticating and loading the remote-processor firmware (modem, ADSP, CDSP, video) — moves from QTEE into OP-TEE, not TF-A. With QHEE replaced by KVM, the old HYP↔QTEE nested-SMC authentication path collapses into the secure world.
